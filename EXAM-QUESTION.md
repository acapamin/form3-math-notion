## 1. Easy Level (Questions 1-10)

These questions focus on fundamental definitions and direct application of basic formulas ($I=Prt$).

### Question 1: Savings Definition

**Question:** Which term describes keeping money in a bank account for future emergencies, a practice generally characterized by high liquidity and lower returns?

**Hint:** Think about whether the money is being actively used to buy assets or simply being stored.

**Correct Answer:** Savings

| Option | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- |
| Investment | Refers to acquiring assets for capital appreciation (higher risk/return). |
| Fixed Deposit | A type of saving, but not the general practice. |
| Savings | ✓ Correct. The practice of setting aside excess money, prioritizing high liquidity. |
| Credit | The ability to obtain goods or money now and pay later (opposite of saving). |
